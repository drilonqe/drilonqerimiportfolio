Ideation

Identify relevant artifacts from the human-centered design process that address the following points.

- What techniques did you use to discover solutions to users’ needs?

For ideation, we used personas to give us more area to think about different kind of students that might use our 4 year planner. We created various personas like a student doing 3-2 engineering, a first year student who is undecided and a student who has recently changed their major. This helped us think outside the box so not thinking about only one type of user but instead think about how the planner should work for different situations and people.

This was really helpful for us since they gave us a clear target of what we should do while we were brainstorming. We found that we were constantly asking ourselves like what would actually help these kinds of students before we even thought about implementing anything, thus the personas helped us go from needfinding to possible solutions that we can put in our program.

- What solution(s) did you ultimate pursue and why did you choose them?

Our solution was to make the 4 year planner site as a place where students don’t just generate schedules but can also add courses they have already taken and generate a possible schedule for the other semesters based on their chosen major. We did this because our personas showed that students can be in very different situations so just merely having a tool that keeps generating 4 year plans is not enough. We tried to make the planner more flexible and good for all of the possible cases.

Our main goal was to make the planner tell the students which courses they took still count towards what they want to major in, and what they need to take to complete everything through a realistic plan. Since this way addressed the main problems from both the interviews and personas, we thought it was a good approach.

Some personas that I came up with

Persona 1: A person who is going to spend only 3 years in Grinnell (i.e., 3-2 Engineering Program)

  • Name: Daniel
  • Major: Physics, preparing for a 3-2 engineering program.

Daniel plans to be in Grinnell for three years and then transfer to an engineering school for two more years. So he must meet both Grinnell requirements and the Engineering School Requirements. This case is a bit trickier for us, but we can tweak our website to have a time you want a certain major completed (e.g., I want a Physics major in 6 semesters), and that is the best way we can help Daniel build a schedule that would most likely resemble what he needs for engineering school.

Persona 2: A person who has just started school at Grinnell

  • Name: Emma
  • Major: Undecided

Emma has just started at Grinnell. She is unsure about what to major in, but has some areas that she might be interested in. She is trying to look at various classes, but also still looks at the credit requirements for graduation. We can help her by having a backup question in case the major is undecided, which asks what potential areas a student might be interested in, and then every time you generate it will pick a major out of those areas and can ultimately help the student decide.

Persona 3: A person who has just changed their major

  • Name: Michael

Michael decided that he does not want to do Computer Science anymore and wants to switch to an economics major. Now, he has to figure out which one of the previous courses he took counts towards his new major and plan how to graduate on time. There will be an option to add courses you have taken so far, and our program will have them unchanged, and simply fill in the remaining part of the schedule with a possible schedule, which will help Michael figure out what he needs to take and when to graduate on time.
